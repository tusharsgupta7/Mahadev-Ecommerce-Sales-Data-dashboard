# 📊 **Mahadev Ecommerce Sales Dashboard — Power BI**

## 📌 **Project Overview**

The **Mahadev Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, customer contribution, product categories, payment methods, and regional sales.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important business KPIs and allows users to explore sales data using **Quarter** and **State** filters.

---

## 📊 **Key Performance Indicators**

The dashboard provides the following major KPIs:

* 🛒 **Total Quantity:** 6K
* 💰 **Total Profit:** 37K
* 💵 **Total Amount:** 438K
* 📈 **Average Order Value (AOV):** 121K

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall sales volume, revenue, profitability, and order-value performance.

---

## 📈 **Sales & Profitability Analysis**

### **🗺️ Amount by State**

The dashboard analyzes sales amount across different states.

Key observations:

* **Maharashtra** contributes the highest sales amount.
* **Madhya Pradesh** is the second-highest contributor.
* **Uttar Pradesh** and **Delhi** contribute comparatively lower amounts.
* The state-wise analysis helps identify the geographical distribution of sales.

This visualization can help businesses understand which regions are generating higher sales and where additional market opportunities may exist.

---

### **📅 Profit by Month**

The monthly profit chart shows how profitability changes throughout the year.

The dashboard displays:

* Positive and negative monthly profit.
* Higher profit levels during **January, February, March, and November**.
* Negative profit during months such as **May, July, September, and December**.
* **November** shows one of the highest monthly profit values.

The monthly trend helps identify periods of stronger and weaker profitability.

---

## 💳 **Payment Mode Analysis**

The **Sum of Quantity by Payment Mode** visualization shows the distribution of orders/quantity across different payment methods.

Payment methods include:

* 💳 **COD — 44%**
* 📱 **UPI — 21%**
* 💳 **Debit Card — 12%**
* 💳 **Credit Card — 12%**
* 💰 **EMI — 10%**

**COD** represents the largest share of quantity, while **UPI** is the second-largest payment method.

This analysis provides insight into customer payment preferences.
